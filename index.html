ALCHEMICAL TILT — 5 LEVEL CORE TEST

Click one sphere, then a touching sphere to swap. Rotate the whole chamber 90° left or right to change gravity. Straight groups of 3+ disappear immediately; surviving spheres fall and can chain.
The chamber is intentionally larger than the sphere pile. Level 1 teaches swapping, Level 2 teaches rotation, then the two mechanics are combined. The colored edge markers rotate with the field so you can track orientation.
